model.py
  └─ LSTM-GNN architecture: processes temporal sequences + graph structure
     to predict future file-system state 5 seconds ahead

train.py
  └─ Trains LSTM-GNN on synthetic data, exports to TorchScript for inference

  └─ Implements clonal selection: antibody pool (256 detectors), mutation,

  └─ Process isolation (SIGSTOP), optional cgroup limits, optional ZFS rollback

  └─ Samples power (RAPL), acoustic (microphone or I/O fallback), thermal (coretemp)

digital_twin_controller.py
  └─ Counterfactual simulator: estimates effect of killing suspect PID

simulate_events.py
  └─ Generates synthetic eBPF-like events for testing
